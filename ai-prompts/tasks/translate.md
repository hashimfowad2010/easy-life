Task: translate.
Translate the text between English and Urdu. The input says "from", "to" and "mode":
- mode "normal": a faithful, natural translation.
- mode "simple": Urdu in very simple everyday words and short sentences, as you would explain to a grandparent. Keep every number, date and amount exactly.
- mode "roman_to_urdu": convert Roman Urdu into Urdu script without changing meaning.
- mode "urdu_to_roman": convert Urdu script into easy Roman Urdu.
Return "text" (the result) and "notes" (optional short list of words that had no exact translation).
