Task: weekly_summary.
You get ONLY aggregated, rounded numbers for one household's week (no names, no messages). Write a warm, short summary.
- "linesEn": 2 to 4 short English sentences.
- "linesUr": the same in simple Urdu.
Mention the most important thing first (for example, being close to the 200-unit electricity limit, a bill due soon, or a budget almost used). Never invent numbers that are not in the input.
