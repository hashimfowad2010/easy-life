# Easy Life v0.9.3: online features switched on

The first build connected to the Easy Life Firebase project (free Spark plan). Installs over v0.9.2 and keeps your data.

## What's new
- **Smart AI works:** Google Gemini (free) reads hard bill, meter and receipt photos and explains papers in simple Urdu, once you turn it on in Settings → Smart AI.
- **Anonymous sign-in:** the app connects without any name, email or phone number.
- **Sharing works:** shop prices you choose to share, anonymous impact counts (only if you opted in) and feedback now reach the project.
- **Crash reports** (Crashlytics) help fix problems.
- Settings no longer says "Online features are not connected".

## What to test on your phone
1. Install `EasyLife.apk` over v0.9.2: your data is still there.
2. Settings: the "Online features are not connected in this build" line is gone.
3. Settings → Smart AI → read the page → "I am 18 or older. Turn on".
4. With internet, scan a real bill: the confirm screen shows "Smart AI (Gemini)". Check the numbers, then save. Settings → Smart AI shows "Today on this phone: 1 of 15".
5. Airplane mode: scan again; it still works and shows "Read on your phone".
6. Urdu Helper → "Simple words" → translate a school notice.
7. Settings → Give feedback → answer and send: "Thank you! Your feedback was sent."
