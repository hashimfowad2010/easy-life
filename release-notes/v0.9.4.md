# Easy Life v0.9.4: installs without "App blocked by Play Protect"

Google Play Protect blocks apps installed from the internet that ask to read SMS or notifications ("App blocked to protect your device"). **`EasyLife.apk` no longer asks for either**, so it installs normally. Installs over earlier versions and keeps your data.

## What changed
- **`EasyLife.apk`** (use this one): no SMS or notification access. For Budget, add bank messages yourself:
  - **Share:** in your Messages app, long-press a bank or wallet SMS → **Share** → **Easy Life**. It goes straight into Budget → Transactions.
  - **Paste:** Budget → Banks and wallets → "Add bank messages" → paste one or many messages (empty line between them) → "Add these messages".
  - OTP messages are still thrown away at once, and everything stays on the phone.
- **`EasyLife-with-SMS.apk`** (optional): the same app plus automatic reading of bank SMS. Many phones block it; only use it if your phone allows it.
- Everything else is unchanged: free Smart AI with Google Gemini, Firebase connected.

## What to test on your phone
1. Download **`EasyLife.apk`** and install it. It should install without the "App blocked" message (you still allow "Install unknown apps" once).
2. Modules → Budget → Banks and wallets: add your bank (name and, if you like, the last 4 digits).
3. In your Messages app, long-press a bank SMS → Share → Easy Life: Budget → Transactions opens with "1 added".
4. Copy two bank SMS, then Budget → Banks and wallets → "Add bank messages" → paste both (empty line between them) → "Add these messages": "2 added".
5. Share a WhatsApp message that is not a bank SMS to Easy Life: it opens in Urdu Helper as before.
6. Share an OTP message: it is not added to Budget.
