# Easy Life v0.9.0: all five modules (pre-1.0)

The first installable release. Everything runs on the phone for free; Smart AI (Gemini/Claude) is built but switched off.

## What's in it
- **Bijli Guard:** bill and meter-photo reading, 200-unit projection and warnings, money at stake, late-reading warning, appliance tips, custom limits.
- **Bills:** scan or type a bill, confirm the numbers, reminders before the due date, "pay by … to save Rs …".
- **Budget:** bank SMS read on the phone (OTPs discarded at once), statement import, review inbox, exclusions with undo, self-transfer and refund pairing, budgets, biometric lock.
- **Urdu Helper:** translate English papers into simple Urdu offline, read aloud, speak, find dates/amounts/to-dos, share from WhatsApp.
- **Smart Shopping:** Roman-Urdu lists, community prices from receipts, retailer search buttons, unit prices, best shop / best split, budget check.
- **ILAP impact report** (CSV + one-page PDF) and a 3-question feedback form.
- **Demo mode** with a fictional family, clearly labelled.

## What to test on your phone
1. Install `EasyLife.apk` (allow "Install unknown apps" when asked).
2. Onboarding: choose اردو and check the whole app flips right-to-left; finish without being asked for any permission.
3. Today → **Try with demo data** → open Bijli Guard: projection, daily allowance, estimated bill, "See saving tips".
4. Scan button → **Try a sample (fictional)** → Sample LESCO bill → Continue: uncertain fields are highlighted; Save → it appears in Bills and updates Bijli Guard.
5. Urdu Helper: type an English sentence → Translate (download the model on Wi-Fi first) → Read aloud. Share a WhatsApp message to Easy Life.
6. Smart Shopping → Weekly groceries → **Compare prices**.
7. Budget → Transactions → exclude one → the month total drops → Undo.
8. Settings → Help the project → **ILAP impact report** → Save one-page PDF and open it.
9. Turn on airplane mode and repeat steps 3, 6 and 7: everything still works.

Full checklist: QA.md in the repository.

## Known limits
- Online features (Remote Config updates, sharing prices, impact upload, feedback upload, crash reports) are off until Firebase is connected.
- Parsers are tuned on fictional samples; use Parser Lab (tap the version number 7 times in Settings) with real bills.
