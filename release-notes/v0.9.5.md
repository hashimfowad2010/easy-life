# Easy Life v0.9.5: your colours, bigger text, cleaner look

Installs over v0.9.4 and keeps your data.

## What's new
- **Choose your colours:** Settings → Appearance → Colour: Teal, Blue, Green, Purple, Orange or Rose, or "Wallpaper" to match your phone's wallpaper (Android 12 and newer). The whole app changes at once, in light and dark mode. Each module keeps its own colour so it's easy to recognise.
- **Text size:** Settings → Appearance → Text size: Normal, Large or Larger, on top of your phone's own font size. Handy for parents and grandparents.
- **Cleaner screens:** Settings grouped into neat cards with icons; a colourful greeting card on Today; softer module tiles.
- **"Impact report"** is the new name for the report in Settings → Help the project.
- Every colour scheme is checked automatically so text stays easy to read.

## What to test on your phone
1. Install `EasyLife.apk` over v0.9.4: your data is still there.
2. Settings → Appearance → Colour: tap each colour; the app recolours straight away. Try Dark mode too.
3. On Android 12 or newer, tap "Wallpaper": the app takes your wallpaper's colours.
4. Settings → Appearance → Text size → Larger: open Today, Bijli Guard and Budget; text is bigger and nothing is cut off.
5. Today: the greeting card shows a sun by day and a moon at night.
6. Settings → Help the project → **Impact report** opens the report (the old "ILAP" name is gone).
