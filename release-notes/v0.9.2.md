# Easy Life v0.9.2: free Smart AI with Google Gemini

Smart AI no longer needs money. It now uses **Google Gemini's free tier through Firebase**: the project stays on the free Spark plan, so nothing can ever be billed. Installs over v0.9.1 and keeps your data.

## What changed
- **Smart AI = Google Gemini (free).** Reads bill, meter and receipt photos better and explains papers in simple Urdu. Each person turns it on in Settings → Smart AI and confirms they are 18 or older (Google's terms).
- **More privacy for the free tier:** besides account, card, CNIC and phone numbers, names and addresses are now hidden too (masked in text, blacked out in photos). Bank SMS and budget data are never sent.
- **Fair sharing of the free quota:** at most 15 Smart AI reads per phone per day; after that, or offline, the app reads on the phone as before.
- **Honest labels:** results say "Smart AI (Gemini)"; the Smart AI page explains that Google may use and review free-tier data.
- Urdu Helper's "Simple words" and Roman-Urdu modes work once Smart AI is on.
- The paid Claude setup stays in the code only as an optional extra; nothing uses it.

## What to test on your phone
Smart AI needs the Firebase setup (see the setup steps). Until then the app says "Smart AI needs the app's online setup" and works fully on the phone.
1. Install `EasyLife.apk` over v0.9.1: your data is still there.
2. Settings → Smart AI: read the page, tap "I am 18 or older. Turn on".
3. With internet, scan a real bill: the confirm screen shows "Smart AI (Gemini)". Check the numbers, then save.
4. Settings → Smart AI shows "Today on this phone: 1 of 15".
5. Turn on airplane mode and scan again: it still works and shows "Read on your phone".
6. Urdu Helper → choose "Simple words" → translate a school notice.
7. Settings → Smart AI → Withdraw consent: Smart AI turns off.
